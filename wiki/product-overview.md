# Product Overview

What MathSolver does, feature by feature. For file locations see [[codebase-map]]. Index: [[index]].

## Positioning

Free, no-login, unlimited AI math solver. Core wedge vs competitors: **full step-by-step solutions are free** (Symbolab/Mathway/Wolfram/Photomath paywall them). See [[growth-strategy]].

## Features

- **AI solver chat** — conversational, streamed, multi-turn (full history sent back for follow-ups). Chats saved to `localStorage`, listed in the sidebar "Recent," deletable. This *is* the app; the solver and chat are the same surface.
- **One-tap solution follow-ups** — the latest completed answer exposes a compact "Keep learning" row. One accessible “Explain a step” picker scales from short answers to 20+ numbered steps; adjacent actions request a same-difficulty problem without revealing its answer or generate/reopen the existing four-question practice quiz. Actions disappear while a response is streaming, quiz failures show a retryable inline error, and saved quizzes on older answers remain reachable.
- **Print worked solutions (live since 2026-09-24)** — the latest completed web answer has a blue "Print / Save PDF" action first among the Keep learning buttons, visible immediately on narrow screens; earlier saved answers retain their own print action below the answer. The completed-answer scroll keeps the actions above the input box. Printing opens the browser's paper/PDF dialog for a clean sheet containing the associated problem, rendered math steps, and final answer. The print renderer loads only after the action is clicked; the chat, controls, and navigation are excluded from print output. It works for guest and saved answers.
- **Video explanations** — the latest completed solution offers “Generate video explanation.” Every verified account can generate private lessons up to its plan's daily allowance per UTC day (Free 2, Plus 10, Unlimited 30; see [[monetization]]). The written solution is used only as an accuracy reference, never as the video storyboard. A schema-v2 pedagogy gate rejects narrated step decks: every supported video must orient the learner, include at least two meaningful non-equation modeling scenes, justify the strategy choice, demonstrate and connect the decisive reasoning, contrast a specific misconception, verify the answer, and generalize the idea. Algebra may remain equation-intensive when the learner sees those equations constructed, transformed, focused, or compared rather than swapped as finished answer cards. The renderer uses those internal teaching scenes to create one continuous 720p/30 fps lesson MP4 with synchronized AI narration, one caption timeline, one poster, and no chapter-by-chapter playback boundary. Every plan must also provide the complete reviewed result as safe MathTex; after the explanation and transfer connection, the renderer replaces the scene with a dedicated `FINAL ANSWER` card and holds it for 3.5 seconds. Captions appear in a separate transcript strip that cannot cover equations. The web player keeps a persistent control rail for play/pause, replay, scrub/time, captions, mute, and full-screen; its visual canvas is height-constrained inside short desktop viewports so the rail and caption strip are visible before playback, while full-screen expands the canvas around those controls. The optional “Practice check” is off by default and can appear only after the complete explanation, never every few seconds. Generation is asynchronous and idempotent; as soon as the server creates the job, a progress attachment appears under the exact assistant answer and becomes the complete player when ready. The synced notebook stores only the private job ID/version—not signed URLs or media—so reopening the chat fetches a fresh authorized manifest. Closing the modal does not lose the attachment. Opening authentication temporarily dismisses the video gate and restores it after auth closes, so the dialogs never overlap. A damaged OCR/input string is teachable when the completed solution explicitly gives one mathematically consistent interpretation; the lesson must disclose that clarification in its opening rather than silently rewriting the question. Invalid generated plans receive one bounded feedback-guided regeneration before an accurately classified `planning_failed` response; a transient missing Manim media fragment receives one bounded render retry in a fresh directory, and an unsupported job receives one refunded external retry. Unsupported or failed renders refund the current daily bucket without affecting another day, completed assets expire after 14 days, and the learner can delete them earlier.
- **Video Library** — `/video-library` is the noindex, account-owned history for up to 24 unexpired generated explanations. It shows signed private posters, duration/chapter metadata, live generation status, failure feedback, and the remaining daily allowance; ready cards reopen the existing continuous explanation without another generation or quota charge. Unlike a public community gallery, problems and videos remain private to the verified owner.
- **Paid plans (live since 2026-10-31)** — `/pricing` is an indexable, conversion-focused page: yearly/monthly toggle (yearly default, "Save 50%"), three cards with Plus highlighted as "Most popular", per-month framing with the monthly list price struck through on yearly, a trust row, a four-question FAQ, and Stripe-hosted Checkout. Plus is $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr, Unlimited $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr; there is no free trial. A guest who picks a plan is sent through the existing sign-in dialog and then straight to checkout. Subscribers see "Current plan" and "Manage billing" (Stripe Customer Portal: cancel at period end, switch price, update card, invoices). Entitlements live in server-only Firestore docs `users/{uid}/entitlements/plan` and `/billing`, written by the Stripe webhook and by a success-page sync; the sidebar shows an accented "Upgrade" item (or "Your plan" for subscribers) and the mobile header a compact Upgrade pill. Daily scan/quiz limits return a neutral 429 that the web chat turns into a translated toast with a "See plans" link (`UsageLimitToast`). The Flutter app sells nothing; a web plan applies to the same account. See [[monetization]].
- **Rich math input** — type plain text, or insert LaTeX via a MathLive field + Σ math keyboard (`InlineMathInput`, `MathFieldInput`, `MathKeyboard`).
- **Photo / PDF OCR** — drag-drop, paste, or file-pick images/PDFs (≤10MB). Extracted equation text is attached to the message. Model: Gemini 3.1 flash-lite via `/api/ocr`.
- **Drawing canvas** — hand-draw a problem (color, line width, undo, clear); exported at 2× as JPEG and sent through the same OCR pipeline (`source:"drawing"`). Almost no competitor offers draw-to-solve on web — currently unmarketed.
- **Practice quizzes** — "Take a Practice Test" on any solution generates 4 MCQs (`/api/practice`); opens in a resizable split panel (desktop) or full-screen (mobile). Per-question answer checking, wrong-answer tracking, running score, on-demand per-question step explanations (`/api/practice/steps`). Questions, choices, and explanations share the solver's Markdown/KaTeX delimiter normalization, with compatibility repair for older saved quizzes containing literal escaped newlines. Finishing a test persists an attempt record with its first-try-correct score.
- **Mistake review** — a first wrong practice answer immediately marks that existing question for review instead of duplicating its content. `/practice-tests` offers a focused queue of up to five due mistakes. Correct reviews advance through 1→3→7→14-day intervals; another miss resets the interval to one day. Review remains attempt-first, includes the existing step help, and lets the learner remove an unsuitable question.
- **Practice library** — `/practice-tests` scans the local-first notebook for saved tests. Completed attempts are stored with each test (up to 20 recent attempts), the library shows the best score, and a compact status shows either the due-review CTA or next review date. The sidebar Practice Tests item also carries a due-count badge. `noindex`.
- **Optional account + synced notebook foundation** — when Firebase is configured, the sidebar offers Google sign-in plus a complete Email/Password journey: registration, activation email, resend/check-verification controls, sign-in, password reset, return-state notices, session status, and sign-out. `/auth/action` provides the branded in-app handler for verification, reset, and recovery links once Firebase templates target it. Unverified Email/Password users remain local-only; verified users merge guest chats, their account cache, and private Firestore chats by ID/latest update. Chat text, OCR text, solutions, generated tests, and practice attempts sync across devices; original uploaded image data URLs stay browser-local. The no-account flow remains unchanged.
- **Draggable calculator** — basic arithmetic (+−×÷), draggable; injects results into the active input.
- **Topic calculators** — `/calculator` links to 43 focused, indexed pages across General Math, Algebra, Precalculus, Trigonometry, Calculus, Linear Algebra, Statistics, and Graphing. Solver-backed pages configure the existing chat for one intent, supply example problems, stream full steps, support photo/PDF/drawing input and follow-ups, and include a formula guide, worked example, common mistakes, FAQs, and related tools. Each chat sends a validated page source so `/api/solve` can apply the correct server-owned operation instruction. See [[calculator-pages]].
- **Graphing calculator** — `/calculator/graphing` is a real client-side plotter, not an SEO-only shell. It supports up to four explicit functions, show/hide and remove controls, editable x/y ranges, pan, zoom, reset, coordinate tracing, an accessible sample-value table, and an "Explain this graph" handoff into the shared tutor chat. Expressions are evaluated by a reusable recursive-descent parser, not `eval`.
- **Pressroom-powered blog** — `/blog` renders the live Pressroom feed server-side with nine articles per page, optional covers/tags/bylines, accessible pagination, empty/error states, and five-minute cache revalidation. `/blog/[slug]` renders trusted rich article HTML, article metadata, BlogPosting structured data, optional author profiles, and a solver CTA. Article authors use `\\(...\\)` for inline LaTeX and `\\[...\\]` for display LaTeX; the server parses only HTML text nodes and emits trusted-off KaTeX with accessible MathML while preserving code/preformatted content, attributes, malformed input, and existing math. The footer and sidebar's small legal-link group link to the blog; live article URLs join the sitemap automatically.
- **Contact support** — `/contact` collects a first name, reply email, and message without requiring an account. Production submissions require the existing Turnstile action/hostname check, apply a small per-instance IP limit and honeypot, validate strict field bounds, and forward plain text to the owner through a server-only Telegram bot. The bot token never reaches the browser.
- **Home navigation** — the MathSolver brand in the desktop/mobile sidebar and the compact mobile header logo both link to `/`. Opening the home link from the mobile drawer also closes the drawer.
- **Flutter mobile app** — the standalone iOS/Android client is publicly downloadable from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-math-solver-mathsolver/id6799652720) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.mathsolver.app); it is not a WebView or a copy of the web chat. Its light-first, three-destination flow has contextual camera/gallery/paste/inline input, crop/rotate, multi-problem worksheet selection, OCR correction, streamed hint-first native-LaTeX steps, independent verification/reporting, and **Check My Work** for the first wrong handwritten line. The learning loop includes follow-ups, quizzes, a warm-up, persisted 1→3→7→14 review, and a combined local-first/Firestore-synced solution/video Library. Native verified Email/Password, Google, and Apple journeys unlock private videos with signed chapters, continuous default playback, optional practice pauses, a caption-safe transcript strip, speed/full-screen controls, offline cache, sharing, and content-free push deep links. Native App Attest/DeviceCheck and Play Integrity feed the versioned `/api/mobile/v1/*` gateway; analytics is opt-in and no-content. The homepage now exposes tracked download buttons in the hero and a dedicated mobile-app section. See [[mobile-app-concept]].

## How solving works

**Web language support (live October 7, 2026):** the solver and practice experience offer 16 languages and right-to-left support. The deployed SEO upgrade provides English at `/`, 15 language homepages such as `/es`, `/ru`, and `/uz`, translated initial HTML and metadata, reciprocal language links, and sitemap coverage. The selector navigates by URL. The FAQ AI-detector link uses a verified matching Detecting AI language page when available and its main homepage otherwise. New answers and quizzes follow the selected language; existing content keeps its original wording. Editorial, account, and video surfaces remain English. See [[web-languages]].

1. `ChatContext.sendMessage` POSTs to `/api/solve`.
2. `/api/solve` calls Gemini 3.1 flash-lite (temp 0.2, max 2000 output tokens) with `MATH_TUTOR_PROMPT`, which forces numbered `**Step N:**` headers, `---` dividers, LaTeX for all math, and a `**Final Answer**` block. For calculator chats, the route validates the `calculator:<slug>` source and appends the matching server-owned topic instruction.
3. The shared Gemini adapter converts provider SSE into a clean `text/plain` stream; the client reads it incrementally.
4. `MessageList` renders markdown via `react-markdown` + KaTeX; `preprocessMathMarkdown` normalizes delimiters while distinguishing number-leading inline math from standalone currency; horizontal rules become styled step dividers.

## What it does NOT have (today)

- Firebase's verification/reset email templates still need to target the production-live custom `/auth/action` route; until that owner-side template setting is changed, emails open Firebase's hosted action page first.
- No bookmarks, folders/courses, topic mastery dashboard, customizable goals, or email capture. The web has no notifications; mobile offers only optional video-ready notifications after a contextual opt-in. The shipped streak is intentionally device-local/account-scoped rather than a Firestore-synced cross-device record; see [[growth-strategy]].
- No ads. Paid plans are live (Stripe Checkout, Plus/Unlimited, no trial; see the **Paid plans** feature and [[monetization]]).
- Visual lessons are built to degrade instead of failing: a flawed plan is repaired, an undrawable visual becomes its formula, a failed narration take falls back to the closest take or the backup voice, and one retryable failure is retried automatically. Only a repeated mathematical objection refuses a lesson. Renderer and web parts both went live September 28, 2026. See [[tech-and-ops]].
- Visual lessons allow 2 generations per verified account per UTC day on Free, 10 on Plus, and 30 (fair use) on Unlimited; the limit is read from the server-written plan entitlement. Free accounts and web guests get 5 scans (photo/PDF/drawing OCR) and 1 practice quiz per UTC day, paid tiers unlimited; anonymous mobile requests are not yet metered. Typed solving, step explanations, printing, review, and sync remain free and unlimited; core solving and full text steps remain no-login.
- The public Flutter client has native Google/Apple/Email sign-in, contextual optional notification consent, signed-in account and private-video deletion, mobile legal links, offline media cleanup, and a server-controlled optional/minimum-version policy. Remaining operations include an APNs key for real iOS notification delivery, broader post-store-install physical-device QA, and moving the deployed App Check gateway from observation to mandatory after valid traffic is measured. Widgets, Pencil scratchpad/drawing, exam packs, and Live Tutor remain roadmap work; they are not presented as shipped.
