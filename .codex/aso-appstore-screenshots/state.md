- Variant 1: full-bleed dark breakout — inspected: yes
- Variant 2: editorial light depth — inspected: yes
- Variant 3: restrained kinetic zoom — inspected: yes
- Selected variant: V2.5 — user approved
- V2.5 definition: V2 editorial-light foundation with V3's larger UI and selective intact-panel breakout depth; phones remain straight throughout the set, while screenshot 4 keeps its tutorial player contained inside the phone.
